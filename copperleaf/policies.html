Copperleaf Tea Co. Policies

Copperleaf Tea Co. is a demo store used to test a customer support agent. All policies on this page are fictional.

Return window

You can return unopened tea within 14 days of delivery. Opened packs cannot be returned for hygiene reasons.

How to start a return

Email hello@copperleaftea.example with your order number. We send a return address within 2 business days. Return postage is paid by the customer unless the item arrived damaged.

Refunds

Refunds go back to the original payment method within 5 business days of the return arriving. We do not offer store credit.

Shipping

We ship to the United Kingdom, Nigeria and Ghana. UK delivery takes 2 to 3 business days and costs £3.95, free over £40. Nigeria and Ghana delivery takes 7 to 10 business days and costs £12.

Damaged items

Report damaged packs within 3 days of delivery with a photo, and we will send a replacement.

Contact

Email hello@copperleaftea.example. We reply Monday to Saturday, 8am to 6pm UK time.
