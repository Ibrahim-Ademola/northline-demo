Frequently Asked Questions

Northline Sneakers is a demo store used to test a customer support agent. All policies on this page are fictional.

Shipping

We ship to the United States and Canada only. Orders are processed within 1 business day.

  • Standard shipping takes 3 to 5 business days and costs $5.95. It is free on orders over $100.
  • Express shipping takes 1 to 2 business days and costs $14.95.

We do not ship to PO boxes on express orders.

Where is my order?

You receive a shipping confirmation email with a tracking link as soon as your order leaves our warehouse. If you have not received it within 2 business days of ordering, check your spam folder, then contact support@northlinesneakers.example with your order number.

Can I change or cancel my order?

You can change the size, change the address or cancel within 2 hours of placing the order. After that, the order goes to the warehouse and we cannot edit it. You can still return it once it arrives, under our returns policy.

How do the sizes fit?

Most styles fit true to size. If you are between sizes, we suggest going up half a size. Each product page has a size guide with measurements in centimetres and inches.

Are the sneakers authentic?

Yes. Every pair is sourced from the brands or from authorised retailers, and comes in its original box. If you ever doubt a pair, contact us and we will review it.

Which payment methods do you accept?

We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, PayPal and Apple Pay. We do not offer cash on delivery or payment plans.

Discount codes

You can use one discount code per order. Codes cannot be combined with sale prices and cannot be applied after an order is placed.

Can you hold a pair for me or restock a sold-out size?

We cannot reserve pairs. For sold-out sizes, use the Notify Me button on the product page and we will email you if the size comes back in stock. Restocks are not guaranteed.

How do I contact support?

Email support@northlinesneakers.example. Our team replies Monday to Friday, 9am to 5pm Eastern Time, usually within one business day.
