Returns and Exchanges

Northline Sneakers is a demo store used to test a customer support agent. All policies on this page are fictional.

Return window

You can return any eligible pair within 30 days of the delivery date. The 30 days start on the day the carrier marks your order as delivered.

What can be returned

  • Sneakers that are unworn, unwashed and in the original box with all tags attached.
  • Gift purchases, within the same 30 days. The refund goes to the original buyer or as store credit to the recipient.

What cannot be returned

  • Items marked Final Sale at checkout.
  • Pairs that show signs of wear, dirt or odour.
  • Pairs returned without the original box.
  • Gift cards.

How to start a return

Email returns@northlinesneakers.example with your order number and the reason for the return. We reply within 1 business day with a prepaid return label and a return authorisation number. Returns sent without an authorisation number may be delayed or refused.

Return shipping cost

Return shipping is free when the item is defective or we sent the wrong item. For all other returns, a flat fee of $7 is deducted from your refund to cover the return label.

Refund timing

Refunds go back to the original payment method. We issue the refund within 3 business days after our warehouse receives and inspects the return. Your bank may need a further 5 to 7 business days to show it on your statement.

Exchanges

You can exchange for a different size of the same style, subject to stock. The replacement pair ships free. If your size is out of stock, we refund the original order in full, with no $7 label fee deducted.

To exchange for a different style, return the original pair for a refund and place a new order.

Store credit option

If you choose store credit instead of a refund, we add a 10 percent bonus to the credit amount. Store credit never expires.

Damaged or defective items

Report damage or defects within 7 days of delivery. Email support@northlinesneakers.example with your order number and clear photos of the problem. We will send a free replacement or a full refund, whichever you prefer.
